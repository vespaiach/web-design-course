Stashling design system

Every sample on this page is drawn by tokens.css and base.css. Change a token, reload, and the sample changes. Switch the system to dark mode to see the dark theme.

Color

Rules use these semantic tokens, never a scale step or a hex value.

  • --color-surface page background
  • --color-surface-alt code blocks, disabled buttons
  • --color-text body text
  • --color-muted secondary text, field edges
  • --color-border card and table lines
  • --color-bar header and footer, with --color-on-bar
  • --color-accent buttons, links, focus, with --color-on-accent
  • --color-danger errors, destructive buttons, with --color-on-danger
  • --color-status-ok, -running, -failed status labels

Type

One family, Inter, and six steps of a fluid scale. Body text is --step-0.

Space

Eight steps on a 4px base. Use --space-inside within a group and --space-between between groups.

  • --space-1 4px
  • --space-2 8px
  • --space-3 12px
  • --space-4 16px, inside
  • --space-5 24px
  • --space-6 32px
  • --space-7 48px, between
  • --space-8 64px

Components

Button

One per view is the primary action. States: hover darkens the fill 15%, pressed moves 1px down, focus draws the ring, disabled loses the fill.

Link

Underlined in text, in --color-link. Every other action on a view is a link.

Compare the plans

Field

Label above, hint under the label, error inside the label after the field is left or the form is sent.

Use 3 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens

Card

A 1px --color-border line, --radius-large corners, padding from the space scale. Cards in a row share rows with subgrid. Used by the features, the plans and the sign-up form.

Table

Lines in --color-border, text aligned to the start, numbers in a column aligned to the end with tabular figures. It scrolls inside its own box on a phone. Used on settings and pricing.
