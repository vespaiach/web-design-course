Questions about the plans

Can Stashling read my files?

No. Your files are encrypted with your own key before they leave your computer, on every plan.

What happens when my storage is full?

New backups stop, and you get an email. Move to the next plan, or keep fewer days of snapshots under Settings.

Can I move from hosted storage to my own later?

Yes. Your repository is yours. Download it from Settings, point Stashling at the new place, and cancel the plan.
